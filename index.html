Biology Study Notes

Photosynthesis

Definition: The process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy (glucose) using carbon dioxide, water, and chlorophyll.

Equation:

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light Energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Key Stages:

  • Light-Dependent Stage (Light Stage):
    • Occurs in the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts.
    • Requires light energy to split water molecules (photolysis) into oxygen, protons (H⁺), and electrons.
    • Produces ATP and NADPH for the dark stage.
  • Light-Independent Stage (Dark Stage / Calvin Cycle):
    • Occurs in the stroma of chloroplasts.
    • Controlled by enzymes (temperature-dependent).
    • Uses CO₂, ATP, and NADPH to synthesize glucose.

Factors Affecting Photosynthesis:

  • Light Intensity: Increases the rate up to a saturation point.
  • Carbon Dioxide Concentration: Primary limiting factor in natural environments.
  • Temperature: Affects enzyme activity in the dark stage (optimum ~25°C–30°C).
